Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TURO, a platform dedicated to fostering student-led mentorship and academic collaboration. By accessing our services, you agree to the collection and use of information as outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: We collect your name and student email address through secure authentication providers.
  • Profile Data: Users provide information regarding academic interests and skills to facilitate mentor and mentee matching.
  • Usage Metrics: We collect basic interaction data and crash reports to maintain app stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Mentorship Matching: To connect students with appropriate mentors based on their academic needs.
  • Account Security: To verify your identity and protect your account from unauthorized access.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze app performance and develop new features for the student community.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Service Providers: We share data with Google Firebase to handle secure database and hosting services.
  • App Community: Your professional profile details are shared with other users to enable mentorship connections.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data if required by law to protect the safety of our users.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Encrypted Storage: All personal data is stored using industry-standard encryption provided by our cloud partners.
  • Access Restrictions: We limit internal access to user data to only what is necessary for app maintenance.
  • Data Integrity: Regular backups are performed to ensure your information remains safe and recoverable.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to review, modify, or permanently delete your personal data directly through your account settings. If you encounter any issues, our developer is available to help you exercise these rights promptly.

7. Children's Privacy

TURO is designed for students and individuals who are at least 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 16.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time to reflect changes in our technology or legal requirements. We will notify you of any major updates via a notice within the TURO application.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@turo.app.
